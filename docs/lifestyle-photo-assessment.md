## Specs
- Keep B&W for mood plates. Shoot product shots in **colour** (black garments read poorly in B&W) and let the site's duotone handle consistency.
- 2400px+ long edge, JPG, named `lifestyle-{product}-{n}.jpg`, with alt text for each.
- Get releases from anyone recognisable; avoid licence plates and bystanders.
