D · Symbols

Terry set in type, legibility first. Strokes take a glyph that follows their direction, solid strokes take heavy glyphs, the background is faint engraving rows. Every cell keeps re-picking its glyph within its weight class, so the type never stops switching while the face stays readable. Hover to stir it faster; flick and the type smears and scrambles red, then settles. Prints top to bottom on load.

Active Theory move: live text scramble over a pointer fluid

A · Wet ink

The current footer engraving, moved to the GPU and laid on a real fluid simulation. Swipe and the lines smear like wet ink, then dry back into the face. Fast strokes pull a red second plate out of register.

Active Theory move: pointer-driven fluid (advect, pressure, project)

B · Loose thread

About 65,000 stitches simulated on the GPU, packed where the face is dark, like the hat embroidery. They fly in and sew the face. Your pointer tears stitches loose along the stroke; springs sew them back.

Active Theory move: GPGPU particles that morph into a form

C · Relief

The engraving rows become a 3D landscape with Terry's face as the terrain. Head-on it reads as the flat engraving; move the pointer down to lean over the ridges. The terrain rises from flat lines on load.

Active Theory move: real-time 3D with pointer camera parallax
